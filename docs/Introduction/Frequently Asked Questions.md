# Frequently Asked Questions
This page contains the most frequently asked questions about TuxBlox.

***

## Is TuxBlox free?
Yes, TuxBlox is completely free and open source.

## What does TuxBlox have?
TuxBlox has many features and improvements, primarily:
- Massive performance improvements applied to Wine
- A FastFlag editor directly within the launcher
- A port of Proton in C/C++ for maximum possible performance and no startup delay
- Direct access to hardware, allowing maximum performance
- Roblox version control in the launcher
- Beginner friendly interface and easy configuration

## When is Roblox Player support expected to come?
We don't have a specific release date yet, we cannot guarantee this, but an estimate would be between late 2026 and early 2027.

## Can I use TuxBlox's compatibility layer for anything else other than Roblox?
Yes, we also allow users to download our compatibility layer separately in our releases page ([tuxblox.net/releases](https://tuxblox.net/releases)), feel free to use it however you want to.
