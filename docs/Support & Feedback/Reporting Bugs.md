# Reporting Bugs

> **NOTE: Bug reports for forks of TuxBlox will not be maintained or triaged by TuxBlox developers.** If you're running a modified/forked version of TuxBlox, please report issues to that fork's maintainers instead. We can only support bugs reproducible on unmodified, official TuxBlox builds.

> **Found a security issue?** Do NOT report it publicly, report it to our email address at **security@tuxblox.net** instead.

## What do I include in my bug report?
Before reporting bugs, please include these in your bug report:

- **Operating system and version** (e.g. Debian 12, Arch Linux, FreeBSD 15.1)
- **Display server** (Wayland or X11)
- **Window manager / desktop environment** (e.g. GNOME, KDE Plasma, Hyprland, i3, sway)
- **GPU and driver** (e.g. NVIDIA 550.xx proprietary, Mesa 24.1, Intel Mesa)
- **TuxBlox version and channel** (e.g. 1.0 stable, 0.2 canary, 0.3 testbuild)
- **Game/experience link or ID**, if the bug happens in a specific Roblox experience
- **Steps to reproduce**, and what you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
- **Logs, screenshots, or a screen recording**, if you have them, these help a lot, check ~/.tuxblox/logs

These will be very useful for the developers to fix, it's not required to include all of them, but please make sure to add any detail you can find if possible.

# How do I report a bug?
You can report a bug through one of these 2 methods, choose whichever one you prefer.

## 1. Through Discord
Join our Discord server ([discord.gg/tfdR4jU4kp](https://discord.gg/tfdR4jU4kp)), and then complete the onboarding steps.

![The support category of the TuxBlox Discord server](https://static.tuxblox.net/images/screenshots/Docs_Discord_BugForum.png)

Navigate over to the **"bugs"** forum in the **"Support"** category.

![The topbar of the bug reporing forum in the TuxBlox](https://static.tuxblox.net/images/screenshots/Docs_Discord_NewPost.png)

Click "New Post" on the top right, add a title to your bug report and details to it, make sure to apply the proper tags aswell!

***

## 2. By creating a GitLab issue
Head over to [gitlab.tuxblox.net](https://gitlab.tuxblox.net/)

![Image of the left side in TuxBlox's GitLab repository](https://static.tuxblox.net/images/screenshots/Docs_GitLab_WorkItems.png)

On the left side, click "Plan", then click "Work Items".

![Image of the topbar in the Work Items tab](https://static.tuxblox.net/images/screenshots/Docs_GitLab_NewItem.png)

Click the "New item" button on the top right side of the tab.

![Image of the New issue popup in GitLab](https://static.tuxblox.net/images/screenshots/Docs_GitLab_NewIssue.png)

In the "New issue" screen, type the title of your bug report and add details to your bug report, after adding details, click "Create issue".

***

Please do note that developers will not be able to immediately look at your bug report, please be patient, as developers might be busy during that time!
