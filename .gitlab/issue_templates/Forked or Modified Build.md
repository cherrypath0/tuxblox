## Please report this to your fork's maintainers

TuxBlox developers do not maintain or triage bug reports for forks or modified
builds of TuxBlox. This includes builds with custom patches, third-party
packaging, or changes to the compatibility layer.

If you believe this is actually a bug in the **unmodified, official** TuxBlox
build, please close this and open a report using the "Bug Report" template
instead.
